Asti - Invade & Defend

by [rogue]Pet

designed for 3v3

Objective: Blue team starts as the attacker, red team
as the defender.  The attacking team must capture
point 1 (in the yard), point 2 (on the upper road) and
point 3 (in the center building with the balcony).

Once all 3 points are capped successfully, teams swap
and play begins again.  Dropped flags return after 30
seconds.

Scoring: Attacking team - 10 points per command
point, defending team gets 5 points every 30
seconds.
