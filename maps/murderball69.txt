               * MURDERBALL 69 *
________________________________________________
made by redux:  02/25/26

CLASSIC FOUR TEAM MURDERBALL ACTION 

Objective:
Take the ball from the center and hold it as long as possible.  

Scoring:
Team and individual points increase every 5 seconds.

Other notes:
Ball carriers drop the ball when they die.
Dropped balls return to the center immediately.

Players are NOT allowed in SPAWN with ball.
Any attempt will result in SUDDEN DEATH.
 
Watch out for the CRUSHER in the backdoor base entrance.
Any team's CRUSHER can be activated by all teams.
CRUSHER resets in 60 seconds.
