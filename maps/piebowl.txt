---------------------------------------
PIEBOWL! - A Dustbowl Alternative
---------------------------------------

For all those pie lovers, you know who you are!

Objective: Blue team must take their pie to
Red team's Bowl to win the game. 
There are two command pies on the way 
which must be secured in order.

Scoring:  Red team - 5 points per minute
           Blue team - 30 points per capture
                       - 254 points for BOWL!
				                      - 314 points total! (pie for the win!)

Other Notes: When a capture point is secured, 
spawn points are revised and a new pie 
appears. Rounds last 25 minutes.

PIE ON!
