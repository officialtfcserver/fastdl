________________________________________________

                   * PUSH 69 *
________________________________________________
made by redux:  02/25/26

Classic football rules, new look for 2026.

Objective: 
Grab the ball from the center of the field.
Run it into the ENEMY base and place it on the 
raised capture point to score a goal.

Scoring:
10 team points per goal.
5 individual points per goal.

Other Notes: 
Ball carriers drop the ball when they die. 
Dropped balls stay on the ground for 30 seconds.
All players are reset whenever a goal is scored.
Buildables are also reset whenever a goal is scored.

Watch out for the CRUSHER in each base.
Each CRUSHER can be activated by either team.
A bonus weapon can be acquired behind the CRUSHER...

Unlike the classic PUSH, all classes are fair game.
