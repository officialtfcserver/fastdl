Castle Arrrrgh!

By: John Fuller
Aka: Drunken Gun, 007

This is an attack/defend style map based in a medieval castle.  
It consists of 3 rounds that the attackers must capture, like the map Dustbowl.
Simply unzip the castleargh.bsp file to your half-life/tfc/maps directory and enjoy!
