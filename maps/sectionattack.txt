One team attacks and the other defends. After 
the attackers succeed in capturing both command 
points, teams will switch and start over.

Attackers receive 5 points per capture, 
defenders receive 1 point per minute while 
gates are up.

Lasers designate protected areas, you may only 
walk through lasers that are your team's color.
