Just unzip into your halflife directory. All the files 
will go into their right spots. If you don't use winzip (shame on you),

.tga files go to tfc/gfx/env
.bsp and .txt files go to tfc/maps
.wav files go to tfc/sound/maya
.mdl files go to tfc/models

MAP BACKGROUND INFORMATION:

There has been a terrible injustice done to the blue team and their ancestral home.
The red team has come to loot all of their artifacts and sell them on the black market.
Luckilly, the blue team has arrived just as the red team started to crate everything up!

================================================================

PLAY INFORMATION:
 
This is a Dustbowl style map. The object is for the blue team to attack the red team and bring their flags to the capture points.
This is done in sequential stages. The first takes blue, from their shrine,into the beginning of the Mayan city, Teotihuacan.
The second is fought amidst a "fortification" of sorts, and the final stage is to capture the Temple of Quetzlcoatl by planting the
blue flag in the observatory and unleashing his godly wrath of flame.The only saving grace for the red team is that blue has been given
a mere 25 minutes to complete their mission.

================================================================

SCORING:

Red Team  - 5 points per minute Temple is held.
Blue Team - 30 points for capturing the entrance to Teotihuacan.
	    - 30 points for capturing the fortification.
	    - 50 points for capturing the Temple of Quetzlcoatl.

Other Notes: Flags return after 60 seconds. Flag carriers move at 75%.
This is probably the truest to the original dustbowl style map to date.
